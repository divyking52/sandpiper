Permanent Resident Visa

A PR or Permanent Residency visa gives you the permanent right to reside in a country, of which you may not be a citizen

02

Visitor Visa

A visitor visa also known as tourist visa, a visitor visa will allow you a temporary entry into a foreign country for spending your holiday

03

Dependent Visa

The dependent visa is a visa category that lets the dependents of a person who may be a permanent resident or a citizen of another country to apply and join him

Student visa is issued to international students who wish to pursue their higher education in a university in foreign countries
